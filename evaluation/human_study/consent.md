## Participant Agreement & Rights

1. **Voluntary Participation**: You are invited to participate in a user evaluation of an evidence-centric knowledge graph reasoning system. Participation is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without penalty.
2. **Procedure**: You will be asked to complete 10 investigative fact-finding and synthesis tasks across synthetic and open-access scientific texts using either the NOESIS interface or manual keyword document search. Each task is capped at 5 minutes.
3. **Data Confidentiality & Anonymization**: All collected data (completion times, submitted answers, and post-study survey responses) will be stored under an anonymized participant code (e.g., `P01`, `P02`). No personal names, email addresses, or identifying biometric data will be published or retained.
4. **Risks and Benefits**: There are no anticipated physical, legal, or psychological risks beyond normal computer screen usage. The study contributes to verifiable, hallucination-resistant scientific intelligence tools.
5. **Data Availability**: Anonymized summary statistics will be published in the project's open-access research repository.
